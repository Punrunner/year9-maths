## Why this matters

It is a very common belief that multiplying always makes things bigger and dividing always makes
them smaller. That is only true for numbers greater than 1 — and prices, measurements and rates
are often decimals less than 1.

## The rule in action

| Calculation | Answer | Compared with 4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $4 \times 2.5$ | 10 | bigger |
| $4 \times 0.1$ | 0.4 | smaller |
| $4 \div 0.5$ | 8 | bigger |
| $4 \div 2$ | 2 | smaller |

So $43.5 \times 0.986 = 45.891$ **must** be wrong without working it out: multiplying by a
number less than 1 has to make 43.5 smaller.

> **Fatima's fraction method** also works: $6.24 \div 0.4 = \frac{624}{100} \div \frac{4}{10} = \frac{624}{100} \times \frac{10}{4} = 15.6$.
