## Why this matters

Calculators are only as good as the buttons you press. A quick estimate — done in your head in a
few seconds — is the best protection against a misplaced decimal point, whether you are checking
a shopping bill, a medicine dose or an exam answer.

## Dividing by small numbers

Estimating $6.72 \div 320$: $7 \div 300 \approx 0.02$. Dividing by a large number gives a small
answer. Estimating $14.08 \div 32.2$: $14 \div 30 \approx 0.5$.

> **Estimates are not exact.** Two different sensible estimates can both be fine. The point is to
> be close enough to catch a big mistake, not to get the exact answer.
