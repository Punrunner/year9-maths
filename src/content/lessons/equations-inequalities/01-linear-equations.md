## Why this matters

An equation is a question written in algebra: *what number makes this true?* Once you can
turn a real situation into an equation and solve it, you can answer questions about ages,
prices, perimeters and much more without guessing.

## Two sides of a balance

Think of the equals sign as the middle of a set of scales. If $x + 3 = 8$, adding 2 to both
sides still balances: $x + 5 = 10$. Subtracting 3 from both sides leaves $x = 5$ — and the
scales still balance.

## Letters on both sides

Move the **smaller** letter term. In $6x - 13 = 7 - 4x$, add $4x$ to both sides rather than
subtracting $6x$ — you avoid negative numbers:

$$10x - 13 = 7 \;\Rightarrow\; 10x = 20 \;\Rightarrow\; x = 2$$

## Useful phrases

| Words | Algebra |
| --- | --- |
| three consecutive numbers | $x,\; x + 1,\; x + 2$ |
| three consecutive odd (or even) numbers | $x,\; x + 2,\; x + 4$ |
| 20 more than $x$ | $x + 20$ |
| twice as much as $c$ | $2c$ |
| 3 is added, then multiplied by 4 | $4(x + 3)$ |

> **Always check.** Put your answer back into the **original** equation. For
> $7 - 3x = 3(2x - 5)$ with $x = \frac{22}{9}$: left side $= 7 - \frac{66}{9} = -\frac{1}{3}$,
> right side $= 3\left(\frac{44}{9} - 5\right) = -\frac{1}{3}$ ✓
