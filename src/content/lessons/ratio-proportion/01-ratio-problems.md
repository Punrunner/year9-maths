## Why this matters

Ratios are everywhere: recipes, mixing paint and concrete, splitting a bill, scale models and
maps. The same short method answers all of them.

## Simplifying first

Divide every part by the highest common factor: $24 : 40 = 3 : 5$. If the quantities have
different units, **make the units the same** before simplifying:

$$22\ \text{kg} : 1500\ \text{g} = 22\,000 : 1500 = 44 : 3$$

## Jadee's mistake

For *"Kamil and Sean share in the ratio $3 : 5$, Sean gets 56 dollars more"*, Jadee divided 56 by
$3 + 5 = 8$. But 56 is not the total — it is the **difference**, which is $5 - 3 = 2$ parts. One part
is $56 \div 2 = 28$, not 7.

> **Always check.** Add the shares to get the total, or subtract them to get the difference —
> whichever the question gave you.
