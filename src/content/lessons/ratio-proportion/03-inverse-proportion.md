  - title: 'Inverse proportion'
    body: 'Two quantities are inversely proportional if, when one is doubled, the other is **halved** (when one is tripled, the other is divided by 3).'
  - title: 'The product is constant'
    formula: 'x \times y = k \qquad \text{or} \qquad y = \frac{k}{x}'
    body: '10 builders take 6 days: that is $10 \times 6 = 60$ builder-days of work, whoever does it.'
  - title: 'Method'
    body: 'Multiply the two known values to find the total amount of "work", then divide by the new value.'
workedExamples:
  - title: 'Builders'
    problem: 'It takes 10 builders 6 days to build a wall. How long would 4 builders take?'
    steps:
      - explain: 'Find the total work.'
        maths: '10 \times 6 = 60 \text{ builder-days}'
      - explain: 'Share it between 4 builders.'
        maths: '60 \div 4 = 15 \text{ days}'
    answer: '15 days'
  - title: 'Speed and time'
    problem: 'Sanjay travels at 50 km/h for 30 minutes. How long would the same journey take at 75 km/h?'
    steps:
      - explain: 'Speed × time is the distance, which stays the same.'
        maths: '50 \times 30 = 1500'
      - explain: 'Divide by the new speed.'
        maths: '1500 \div 75 = 20 \text{ minutes}'
    answer: '20 minutes'
  - title: 'A formula'
    problem: '$m$ is inversely proportional to $b$, and $m = 20$ when $b = 36$. Write a formula for $m$ and find $m$ when $b = 60$.'
    steps:
      - explain: 'The constant product.'
        maths: 'k = 20 \times 36 = 720'
      - explain: 'Write the formula.'
        maths: 'm = \frac{720}{b}'
      - explain: 'Substitute.'
        maths: 'm = \frac{720}{60} = 12'
    answer: '$m = \frac{720}{b}$; $m = 12$'

## Why this matters

More workers finish a job sooner. A faster car makes the same journey in less time. Wider buckets
fill a tank in fewer trips. These are all inverse proportion — and the same one-line method solves
every one.

## Direct or inverse?

| Situation | Type |
| --- | --- |
| 6 taps fill a tank in 20 minutes | inverse — more taps, less time |
| 30 pounds = 41.70 dollars | direct — more pounds, more dollars |
| 4 friends clean a house in 1 hour | inverse |
| Rectangles with the same area: length and width | inverse — $l \times w$ is constant |

> **Check the direction.** Before calculating, ask: *should the answer be bigger or smaller?*
> 4 builders instead of 10 must take **longer**, so 15 days is sensible; 2.4 days is not.

## Two changes at once

*10 minutes to fill a 200-litre tank with 4 taps; how long for 500 litres with 2 taps?* Change one
thing at a time. With 4 taps, 500 litres takes $10 \times \frac{500}{200} = 25$ minutes (direct). With
2 taps it takes twice as long: 50 minutes (inverse).
